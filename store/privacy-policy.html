Harambee Tracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Harambee Tracker helps treasurers keep a record of contributions to a fundraiser (Harambee). This policy explains what the app does with information.

Information stored by the app

The app stores the information you enter or import: Harambee details (name, appeal text, target, send-to numbers), contributor names, phone numbers, amounts, M-Pesa transaction codes and message text, pledges, members lists, notes and an activity history. This information is stored only on your device.

What we collect

We do not collect any information. The app has no server and no analytics or advertising tools, and it does not send your data anywhere.

Sharing

Information leaves your device only when you choose to share it, for example a WhatsApp update, a PDF or spreadsheet export, a public campaign page or a backup file. You choose where it goes through your phone's share menu. Privacy settings let you shorten or hide contributor names and amounts in anything you share. Phone numbers are never included in shared updates.

SMS (full edition only)

The edition distributed outside Google Play can read M-Pesa confirmation messages from the sender "MPESA" to record incoming payments. It ignores all other messages. The Google Play edition does not request SMS access.

Backups

You can save a backup file to a location you choose. If Android backup is enabled on your phone, Android may include the app's data in your personal Google backup. That is controlled by your phone's settings and Google's own policies.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Backup & restore → Delete all, or uninstall the app. Backup files you saved elsewhere are under your control.

Children

The app is intended for adults managing group contributions.
