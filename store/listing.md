**Short description** (max 80):
Track Harambee contributions, pledges and totals, and share WhatsApp updates.

**Full description** (max 4000):

Collecting contributions for a burial, medical bill, wedding, church project or welfare group on your own M-Pesa number? Harambee Tracker keeps an accurate list and running total for you, and writes the WhatsApp update in seconds.

✅ ADD CONTRIBUTIONS FAST
• Share or paste M-Pesa confirmation messages into the app. The name, amount, code and time are filled in for you
• Paste the contribution list already going round the WhatsApp group. Names with ✅ count as paid, the rest as pledges
• Add cash, bank or other payments, and pledges, by hand

🔒 NOTHING COUNTED TWICE
• Every M-Pesa transaction code is recorded once. Pasting the same message again changes nothing
• Warnings for entries that look like duplicates, and for possible double payments

📲 WHATSAPP-READY UPDATES
• Full numbered list with ✅, total, target and balance, in the format groups already use
• New since last update, a single "received with thanks" post, milestone posts (Halfway there! 🎉) and a final report
• One tap to share to the WhatsApp group

📊 FOR THE TREASURER
• Dashboard: total, balance, contributors, daily collections, payment methods and top contributors
• Contributor list: rename, merge duplicates, mark anonymous, send thank-yous and pledge reminders
• Several collectors on different numbers, with a total for each
• Members groups for welfare and church groups: see who hasn't contributed yet
• Corrections need a reason, and every change is kept in the activity history
• Export to spreadsheet (CSV) and PDF, a progress card for WhatsApp Status, and a campaign web page

🛡️ PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your records stay on your phone. Nothing is uploaded
• Choose how names appear in shared lists: full name, first name + initial, initials or no names. You can also hide amounts
• App lock with fingerprint or PIN
• Backup to a file you control (Google Drive, WhatsApp) and restore on any phone

Harambee Tracker does not send or receive money. It is a record-keeping tool for treasurers and committees.
